## About

I build practical, privacy-first software — from custom ERP systems to SaaS platforms that help small businesses get online. Based in Johannesburg, working across product design, architecture, and deployment, with a bias toward solutions people can actually put into production.

**Principle:** technology should serve the people using it — without trading away their control or their data.

<br/>
